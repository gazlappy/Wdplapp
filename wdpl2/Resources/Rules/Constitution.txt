CONSTITUTION

1. Name
The league shall be known as the '[League Name]'.

2. Objects
The object of the league is to organise and promote the playing of pool between member teams, in a friendly and sporting spirit.

3. Membership
Membership is open to any team that enters for the season, pays the league fees set at the Annual General Meeting, and agrees to abide by this constitution and the league's match rules.

4. Committee
The league shall be managed by a committee consisting of a Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer and such other officers as the Annual General Meeting decides. Committee members are elected at the Annual General Meeting and serve until the next.

5. Annual General Meeting
The Annual General Meeting shall be held before the start of each season. At least 14 days' notice of the meeting shall be given to every team. The AGM will receive the committee's reports and accounts, elect the committee, and consider any proposals received.

6. Eligible Voters
Eligible voters at a general meeting are the committee and one representative of each team entered for the forthcoming season.

7. Proposals
Proposals to change this constitution or the match rules must be sent to the Secretary in writing at least 7 days before the meeting at which they are to be considered. A change requires a two-thirds majority of those voting.

8. Finance
The league's funds shall be held in an account in the league's name. Payments require the approval of two committee officers. Accounts shall be presented at each Annual General Meeting.

9. Discipline
The committee may take action against any team or player that brings the league into disrepute or breaks its rules. Anyone affected has the right to be heard before a decision is made.

10. Dissolution
The league may be dissolved only at a general meeting called for that purpose. Any remaining funds shall be distributed as that meeting decides.
