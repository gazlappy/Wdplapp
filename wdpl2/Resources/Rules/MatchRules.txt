[League Name]
MATCH RULES

1. Game Rules
Frames are played to the rules of the governing body the league adopts (for example the EPA rules), as in force at the start of the season, except where these match rules say otherwise.

2. Home Table
Each team must provide a home pool table, in playable condition, available for its home fixtures.

3. Team Size
A team must have a minimum of [5] registered players and a maximum of [12].

4. Match Format
A match consists of [15] frames. No player may play more than [3] frames in one match. Captains must exchange team sheets before the first frame is broken.

5. Start Time
Matches start at [8.00 p.m.]. A team not ready to play within [15] minutes of the start time forfeits frames as the committee sets out.

6. Scoring
A team scores one point for each frame won, plus any bonus points the league awards for winning the match. Results must be submitted by both captains within [48] hours.

7. Postponements
A match may be postponed only by agreement of both captains and with notice to the Secretary. Postponed matches must be played by the date the committee sets.

8. Player Registration
Players must be registered with the league before they play. A player may be registered for only one team in a season unless the committee agrees a transfer.

9. Conduct
Players and spectators are expected to behave in a sporting manner. Disputes on the night should be settled by the two captains; anything unresolved is referred to the committee, whose decision is final.

10. Cups and Competitions
Rules for knockout cups and individual competitions are published separately by the committee before each competition begins.
